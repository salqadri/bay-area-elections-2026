YOUR PRIVACY

An address, not an account.

There is no sign-in, analytics or address storage in this guide. Your entered address and lookup results stay in memory in this browser tab and disappear when you reload or leave.

When Google suggestions are enabled, what you type is sent to Google Places to offer matching addresses. Your selected address is then sent to the U.S. Census Bureau to identify geographic districts. Manual entry sends only the submitted address to Census.

Neither an address nor coordinates are added to page URLs or printed guides. Google and Census apply their own privacy policies when handling requests.

ABOUT THIS GUIDE

A simpler way through your ballot.

The recommendations and candidate blurbs are reproduced from the published Bay Area Ballot Research Assistant. The guide does not generate new assessments, summaries or recommendations.

Recommendations reflect that project’s published editorial framework, with a focus on Palestine, Gaza and U.S. aid to Israel. They are not an evaluation of every issue. A missing recommendation does not mean a candidate is opposed.

Only confirmed elected-office contests in the five covered Bay Area counties appear. Qualified write-ins are labeled. Ballot measures and unconfirmed filers are outside this guide. An unresolved local district is shown as a possibility, not as a confirmed race for your address.

Office sections follow California Elections Code 13109. Your county may vary the order of local offices. Compare with your official ballot before voting.

TERMS OF USE

Use this as a guide.

This independent guide republishes existing editorial recommendations and summaries. It is not an official ballot or an elections office. Check your registration, eligibility, deadlines and actual ballot with your county elections office.

Address matching and third-party services may be unavailable or incomplete. No account or address retention is required to use this site.

When using Google address suggestions, the Google Maps Platform Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy apply.
