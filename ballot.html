      How this estimate works

      We match the geographic areas returned by the U.S. Census Geocoder to the contests in this research dataset. When a boundary or district membership cannot be resolved, we retain plausible alternatives and label them. A geography match does not confirm registration, voter eligibility, or that a contest will appear on your official ballot.

      Some contests remain unresolved in the underlying research. Census district boundaries may lag changes used for the 2026 election. Where current boundaries cannot be verified, results are intentionally broader. Use the individual race links to inspect candidates, sources, and research notes.
