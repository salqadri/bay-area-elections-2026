## Organization context and next evaluations

The [club’s stated mission](https://www.acjdc.org/about-acjdc) includes support for Israel as a democratic, inclusive and secure Jewish state. This is sourced policy context, not an inference from Jewish identity. The owner requests low weight for an endorsement alone. Candidate questionnaire answers are separate direct evidence; an endorsement does not establish agreement with every club position.

## Reproduction and checks

`responses.json` retains the reviewed source URLs, document IDs, text-extraction hashes, source/current offices, topic mapping and concise summaries. The [registered review](../../reviews/acjdc-questionnaires-2026-11-02.json) binds each added evidence/endorsement record to those captures. Source documents remain on the club’s Drive; they are not republished in this repository.

`scripts/research_acjdc.py` drafts updates from a fresh captured endorsement page plus a reviewed response packet. Only exact names or recorded middle-name aliases, matched to reviewed office identities, are accepted. Unmapped candidates and changed office mappings remain held. Use `reviewed_updates.py preview/apply` to inspect/adopt a new review; never overwrite an already registered review. Run `merge_gaza_evidence.py --sync-published`, then revalidate the plan with `--refresh-source-binding` and rebuild the readers.

`scripts/check_acjdc.py` checks phase/rank preservation, unchanged N/A questions, cross-office identity, organization context and unchanged CAIR tiers. The full release gate includes it.
